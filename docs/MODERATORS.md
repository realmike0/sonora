# Moderators 🛡️

Moderators are Sonora users that the admin has chosen to keep the comments clean. They show up in comments with a small shield next to their name. The shield changes with **how long they have been moderators**.

| Time as moderator | Shield | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 1 month | **New** (slate grey) | a dot |
| 1 month | **Sprout** (green) | a leaf |
| 3 months | **Bronze** | one chevron |
| 6 months | **Silver** | two chevrons |
| 1 year | **Gold** | three chevrons |
| 2 years | **Sapphire** (blue) | a star |
| 5 years | **Amethyst** (purple) | a diamond |
| 10 years | **Ruby** (red) | a crown |
| More than 10 years (from year 11) | **Senior** (black, gold ring) | a golden crown |

The **admin** has its own orange shield with a star. The admin is the account whose Sonora ID starts with `sid_6aee`. Nobody else is a moderator until the admin gives them the tag from the Sonora Admin app.

## What moderators can do

- Remove a comment.
- Suspend a user for up to **7 days** (the admin can suspend for longer).
- Send a complaint (a warning) to a user.
- Report a user or comment to the admin. The admin sees it in the Sonora Admin app.

Moderators cannot ban, cannot act on other moderators or on the admin, and cannot see tickets.

## How reports count

When someone reports a comment it gets a weight: a normal user counts **1**, a moderator **2**, the admin **99**. A comment is hidden when the total reaches **3**, so an admin report hides it right away.
