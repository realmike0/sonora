## Sonora 0.90-beta (part 5)

**New**
- Inbox in Settings (next to Manage): messages from the team and tickets with the admin (chat)
- When you open Sonora a banner drops from the top with a complaint, suspension or ban, with a button to open a ticket
- System notification in the same style as the headphones one: yellow for a complaint, grey for a suspension, red for a ban
- Moderators: tagged by the admin, they can remove comments, suspend (up to 7 days), send complaints and report to the admin
- A small shield next to moderators shows how long they have been moderators (see docs/MODERATORS.md)
- Reports weigh more when they come from moderators, and the admin's report hides a comment right away
- Sonora Admin: tickets chat, reports from moderators, moderator tag

## Part 4

**New**
- Sonora Admin: a small separate app to manage users, comments and dedications (private build)
- Users can be banned, suspended for N days, or sent a complaint (shown inside Sonora and sent by email if verified)
- Suspended accounts can't comment or dedicate

## Part 3

**New**
- Comments with Sonora ID: a "Sonora" tab next to the YouTube one, checked by AI before publishing (report / delete your own)
- Upcoming albums with a live countdown
- Favorite artists (heart on the artist page) and a Favorites page in Library
- Mashup (Explore): pick up to 4 songs, choose the part of each, play them in a row

## Part 2

**New**
- Settings reworked: animated header (notes pop out, the cookie spins and slows down, then settles into your profile picture)
- Settings pages: Style, Player, Advanced, Languages, Info (+ Manage for your account)
- Style: dynamic colors on/off, round color picker when off, light / dark / follow device
- New Material 3 loading indicator everywhere
- Pages now slide in from the right, like stock Android 14+ (no bounce)
- Blur at the bottom of the player cover
- Faint Sonora logo tinted with the cover color, in the player and in the lyrics image

## Part 1

**New**
- Sonora Framework, a separate app with no icon that manages Sonora ID accounts (installs with one tap from Sonora)
- Change password
- Connected devices (and disconnect, with password check)
- Change email (with a code)
- Multiple accounts, also from Android's account settings
- Profile picture, set from the Framework
- New login emails with device name, place and IP

**Sonora ID**
- Logging in from a new device sends you an email
- Changing the password signs out all other devices
