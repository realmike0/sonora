    Listen

    • Background playbackKeeps playing with the screen off, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
    • Smooth mixingSongs blend into each other, and there is an equalizer.
    • Downloads and cacheLong-press a song and choose Download. Set the cache from 1 GB to unlimited.
    • Audio hapticsYour phone vibrates to the beat, with adjustable sensitivity.

    Read along

    • Synced lyricsLine by line, from 5 sources, with translation.
    • Lyrics imageShare a line as a picture tinted with the cover's colors.
    • AI ratingA score for artists and albums, plus daily picks and a queue remix.
    • SearchFind songs and artists by typing or by voice.

    Share

    • Sonora IDOne account for your favorites and playlists on every phone.
    • DedicationsDedicate a song to someone. Everyone with a Sonora ID can see it.
    • CommentsTalk about a song, checked by AI and by moderators.
    • WrappedYour year in music. Tracking is optional and off until you say yes.

    Everywhere

    • Material YouThe whole interface takes its colors from your wallpaper.
    • Phones and tabletsTwo panes on tablets, an always-on display mode in landscape.
    • Headphones and carWhen they connect you get a notification with Start listening.
    • 7 languagesItaliano, English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Türkçe and Shqip.
